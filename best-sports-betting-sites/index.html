How we rank sports betting sites

Our sportsbook scoring uses six criteria, weighted for what matters to a New Zealand punter rather than a British or American one.

  • Market depth on NZ-relevant sport (25%). Rugby union down to NPC level, rugby league including the Warriors, netball, and Black Caps cricket. A book with 40 football leagues and no NPC is not a New Zealand betting site.
  • Price and margin (25%). We sample head-to-head and handicap prices across rugby, NBA, football and tennis and calculate the implied margin. A 2% difference here outweighs any welcome offer over a year.
  • Withdrawal speed (20%). Timed by us from request to funds landing, separately for crypto, e-wallet and NZD bank.
  • In-play quality (15%). Refresh rate, bet rejection rate and how the interface behaves on a patchy mobile connection.
  • Banking (10%). Native NZD balances, which methods clear from New Zealand banks, and whether withdrawals fail.
  • Trust and transparency (5%). Verifiable licence number, named operating company, and withdrawal terms that do not reserve unlimited discretion.

Full detail is on our review methodology page, including what disqualifies an operator outright.

Play safe

Gambling should cost you time, not your week’s wages

Every site on this page is built to make money from you over the long run. That is not a scandal, it is arithmetic — the house edge is published and it does not move. The only thing you control is how much you put through it.

Set a deposit limit first

Every casino here lets you cap daily, weekly or monthly deposits from the account settings. Do it before your first deposit, not after a bad night.

Use reality checks

A pop-up every 30 or 60 minutes telling you how long you have been playing sounds trivial. In practice it is the single most effective tool these sites offer.

Self-exclude if you need to

Time-outs run from 24 hours to six weeks. Self-exclusion runs six months or longer and cannot be reversed on request. Both are free and take two minutes.

Free help, 24/7

The Gambling Helpline is free and confidential on 0800 654 655. The Problem Gambling Foundation offers free counselling nationwide.

Sports betting NZ: your questions answered

What is the best sports betting site in NZ?

For overall market depth and rugby coverage, Rooster Bet was the strongest offshore book we tested, scoring 9.0. For in-play betting, Bet&Play is clearly ahead. For New Zealand thoroughbred and harness racing, TAB NZ is both the authorised operator and, on the merits, the better product because the pools are domestic. Read our online betting guide for the legal context before opening any offshore account.

Which betting site has the deepest rugby markets?

Rooster Bet, by a clear margin among offshore books. It was the only one that consistently priced NPC matches alongside Super Rugby Pacific and the Rugby Championship, and the only one offering handicaps, first try-scorer and same-game multis across the full New Zealand domestic calendar rather than tests alone.

Can I bet on the All Blacks from New Zealand?

Yes. All Blacks tests are covered by TAB NZ and by every offshore book that accepts New Zealand registrations. Placing the bet is not an offence for you. Prices on headline tests are competitive across the board because liquidity is high; the difference between books shows up on the derivative markets — handicaps, margins, try-scorers — rather than the head-to-head.

Do betting sites accept NZD?

Many do. Rooster Bet, Bet&Play, Kingdom, Smash, Rivo, MadCasino and Lucky Vibe all hold New Zealand dollar balances. Gunsbet is euro-denominated, which costs roughly 2–3% in conversion each way. Always check the account currency before depositing — the spread is usually larger than the price difference between books.

What is the fastest-paying betting site for Kiwis?

Kingdom at two to four hours on crypto, with Rooster Bet and Bet&Play close behind at two to eight hours. NZD bank withdrawals take one to three business days everywhere. Unverified accounts are the main cause of delay, not the payment method.

Is there a betting site with no wagering on the free bet?

Not among the books accepting New Zealanders in 2026. The closest is Bet&Play at 5x turnover with a minimum odds requirement of 1.80, which is genuinely modest. Treat any book advertising a ‘no wagering’ free bet with scepticism and read the maximum-conversion clause before you accept it.
