## 7. What we deliberately do **not** target

- **"free pokies" / "free slots no download"** — huge volume, near-zero commercial value,
  and it drags the site's topical centre away from real-money intent.
- **"casino no verification" / "no KYC casino"** — attracts the wrong audience and signals
  low trust to both Google and operators.
- **"casino tricks" / "how to win at pokies" / "winning strategy"** — we cannot write these
  honestly, and writing them dishonestly undermines every EEAT signal on the site.
- **Bonus-code hunting terms for brands we do not carry.**
- **Under-18 adjacent or game-adjacent terms** (character names, "free to play").
