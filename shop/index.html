    Answers

    Ordering online: common questions

    How does ordering online work?

    Add what you want to the cart and press Pay now. You pay on Stripe’s secure page, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal or Link, and enter your delivery address there. We email to confirm and deliver in 7 to 10 days. If an item turns out to be unavailable we refund it in full. Bulk packs (sold by the maker’s minimum order, e.g. 500) are different: their price excludes delivery, so add them to your cart and press Request my total; we reply with the total including delivery and a secure Stripe invoice.

    How long does delivery take?

    Delivery takes 7 to 10 days from when you pay, anywhere in New Zealand. On most single items it is free: the price already includes it. Clothing, boots and bags marked “+ delivery by weight” add delivery in the cart once you choose your city: it depends on the destination and the weight of those items (from NZ$30.11 for the first kg). On bulk packs it depends on the size of the order and where it is going, so we quote it with your total.

    How do I pay?

    Pay at checkout through Stripe’s secure payment page, by Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Link, plus local payment methods where your country and currency support them. If anything you have paid for turns out to be unavailable, we refund it in full. All payments go through Stripe: we never see or store your card details, and we never ask for them by email or phone.

    Is my cart saved?

    Your cart is kept in this browser on this device until you send the order or clear it. We never see it until you send us the order email. See our cookie policy.
