    Answers

    Common questions

    How does ordering online work?

    We are confirming prices with our suppliers, so for now every product is price on request. Add what you want to your enquiry list and send it with your delivery address. We reply, usually the same working day, with prices, stock and how to pay. Nothing is charged until you have agreed the price.

    How long does delivery take?

    7 to 10 days from when we confirm your order, anywhere in New Zealand.

    How much is delivery?

    Nothing extra. Every price already includes delivery anywhere in New Zealand, so the price you see is the price you pay.

    Do you ship overseas?

    No. We deliver within New Zealand only.

    How do I pay?

    Once we confirm stock, we email you a secure Stripe payment link to pay by card online, or our account number and your order reference to pay by bank transfer. You can also pay by Visa or Mastercard over the phone on 06 765 7248. We never ask for card details by email, and our bank account number only ever comes in our reply to your order.

    Are your prices in New Zealand dollars?

    Yes. Every price is in NZ dollars and includes GST and delivery. We are confirming prices now; send an enquiry and we will quote you.

    How do I know what size to order?

    Sizing varies by maker, so put your usual size, and for gloves your hand measurement, in the order notes. We check it against the product and confirm the fit with you before you pay. See the Apparel questions for how to measure a hand.

    Can I return something?

    If an item is faulty or not as described, we put it right as the Consumer Guarantees Act requires. Ring 06 765 7248 or email editor@magnumsports.co.nz. If you have simply changed your mind, get in touch and we will tell you what we can do.

    What is tactical gear?

    Outdoor equipment built the way military and emergency-service gear is: tough nylon, reinforced stitching, and modular attachment such as MOLLE, so you can carry exactly what a day needs. Hunters, trampers, cyclists, tradespeople and anyone who is hard on their kit use it for the same reason: it lasts.
