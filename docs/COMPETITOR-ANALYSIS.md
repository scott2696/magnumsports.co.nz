## 2. What the strong competitors do well

### Content strategy and topical coverage
- **Segmentation by player archetype, not just a top-10.** casinos.com carves the market into
  VIP, fast-payout, low-deposit, new casinos, then by game type (pokies, blackjack, roulette,
  live dealer) and by bonus type (no-deposit, free spins, cashback). Each becomes an internal
  link and a separate ranking asset. This is the single highest-leverage pattern on page one.
- **A published rating methodology.** All three serious competitors have a "how we rate"
  section with 5–6 named criteria. None publishes the *weightings*.
- **Legal currency as a differentiator.** bettingtop10.co.nz is the only competitor that
  correctly describes the 2026 licensing position, cites the DIA, names the 15-licence cap and
  quotes the online gambling duty rate. This is also the page Google appears to trust most for
  NZ-intent queries.
- **Length.** The winners run 4,500–6,000 words. The 1,200-word global template ranks on
  authority, not on merit.

### Page structure and heading hierarchy
A consistent pattern across every ranking page:

```
H1  Best Online Casinos NZ 2026
H2  Top N Online Casinos — Reviewed & Ranked      ← toplist, above the fold
H2  Find the Right Casino for You                 ← segmentation cards
H2  Bonuses at NZ Online Casinos
H2  Online Casino Games
H2  NZ Gambling Laws, Licences & Safety
H2  How We Rate NZ Casinos
H2  How to Sign Up
H2  Payment Methods
H2  Staying Safe / Responsible Gambling
H2  Meet the Author
H2  FAQ
```

The toplist is **always** the first content element after the H1 and intro. Nobody makes the
reader scroll past prose to reach it.

### Conversion and UX elements used repeatedly
- Ranked cards, not tables, for the primary toplist — logo, bonus headline, rating out of 10,
  payment icons, CTA, expandable detail.
- Payment-method icon rows inside each card (strong trust signal, cheap to implement).
- "18+ / T&Cs apply / Play Responsibly" micro-copy under every CTA.
- Sticky or repeated CTAs down the page.
- Anchor-link jump menus to bonus types and game types.
- Last-updated timestamp near the top, often to the minute (`17th Aug 2026, 02:37 PM`).

## 4. Keyword and SERP targeting patterns observed

- **Title formula** is near-universal: `Best Online Casino[s] NZ {year} | {qualifier}`. Almost
  everyone uses it, which means almost nobody differentiates on CTR.
- **Year in title** is mandatory for this query class. Pages without it under-perform.
- **FAQ schema** is deployed by all three serious competitors; PAA boxes are dominated by
  legality, tax and payout-speed questions.
- **Review/AggregateRating schema** is *not* widely deployed on the hub pages — an opening.
- **ItemList schema** on the toplist: used by casinos.com only.
- SERP features present for the head term: **People Also Ask**, **sitelinks** on brand
  navigational queries, **featured snippet** intermittently for "is online gambling legal in
  New Zealand" (currently held by a paragraph snippet).
- **No video, no image pack** for the head term — those surfaces are uncontested.

## 5. Strategic read

The head term is defended by two things: **domain authority** (gambling.com, casino.guru) and
**parasite placements**. Neither is defended by content quality, and the parasite placements
are not defended by anything durable.

The winning line is therefore not "write another top-10 list." It is:

1. **Be the only page that is factually current on NZ law** — both the casino licensing regime
   *and* the 2025 betting monopoly. This is a genuine, checkable differentiator and it is the
   basis on which a small site earns trust for a YMYL query.
2. **Publish a dataset nobody else has** — 168 timed withdrawals. Original data is the strongest
   EEAT signal available to a new domain and it is citable.
3. **Publish the arithmetic** — wagering in dollars, free spins in dollars, FX spread in dollars.
   Every competitor states terms; none translates them.
4. **Publish negative findings.** A site that criticises an operator it links to reads as
   independent in a way no disclosure paragraph achieves.
5. **Build the cluster, not the page.** casinos.com ranks because the hub is supported by 20+
   internal category pages. We launch with 42.
