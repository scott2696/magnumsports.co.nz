    Description

    The Double Tactical Mag Pouch Molle Vest Accessory Bag CS Outdoor is a MOLLE-compatible magazine pouch. Magazine pouches hold mags securely on a belt, chest rig or plate carrier and release them fast when you need them. They suit airsoft, range and training loadouts, and double as handy pouches for a phone, torch or multi-tool.

    Features

    • MOLLE-compatible
    • Material: 600d High-Density Oxford Fabric
    • Size: 5.9"×1.18"×5.9"
    • Weight: 120g
    • Available in black, od green, coyote brown, cp camo and acu camo (see the photo for this listing)

    Good for

    • Airsoft and paintball loadouts
    • Range and training kit
    • Chest rigs and plate carriers
    • Holding torches or tools

    Check the calibre and size in the name (9mm, 5.56, 7.62) against the magazines you use.
