    How ordering works

    Add what you want to your cart and send us the order. We reply to confirm stock and how to pay before anything is charged, and deliver in 7 to 10 days. Every price includes delivery anywhere in New Zealand and GST. Pay now by card through Stripe’s secure checkout: Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Or send us an order request and pay by bank transfer or by card over the phone on 06 765 7248. If anything you have paid for turns out to be unavailable, we refund it in full.
