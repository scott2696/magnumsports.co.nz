    Description

    The Tactical Shemagh Scarf 110X110cm Cotton Outdoor Large Square in black is a shemagh. A shemagh is a large square cotton scarf worn as a neck wrap, face cover or head wrap. It keeps sun, wind, dust and sandflies off, and doubles as a sweat rag, sling or pot holder.

    Features

    • Material: Cotton
    • Size: 110X110cm
    • Also: Breathable Lightweight with Tassels

    Good for

    • Sun and wind protection
    • Hunting and tramping
    • Dusty tracks and dirt bikes
    • Camping

    To tie a shemagh as a neck scarf, fold it corner to corner into a triangle, place the point at the front and wrap the ends around your neck. A shemagh and a keffiyeh are the same kind of scarf; shemagh is the name most used in outdoor and military gear.
